Frequently asked questions

Does CostumeScout.store sell costumes directly?

No. CostumeScout.store is an independent discovery and affiliate website. Shopping buttons open a third-party retailer that handles product availability, pricing, payment, shipping, returns and customer service.

Are the featured costumes officially licensed?

Featured collections may include officially licensed products offered by the partner retailer. Always verify the licensing statement on the individual product page before purchasing.

What costume categories can I browse?

You can browse Halloween costumes, cosplay outfits, wigs, masks, makeup, props, party accessories, handbags, purses, clutches, couples costumes, kids costumes, pet costumes and vintage styles.

How do affiliate links affect the purchase price?

CostumeScout.store may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through affiliate links. This normally does not add an extra charge to the shopper.

Who handles orders and returns?

The third-party retailer handles orders, payments, shipping, returns, exchanges and customer support under the retailer's own policies.
